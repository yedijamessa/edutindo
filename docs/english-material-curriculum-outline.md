# English Material Curriculum Outline

This outline lists the current English curriculum structure already planned in the repo, based on the IELTS-aligned blueprint in:

- `docs/english-curriculum-from-ielts-prep.md`
- `lib/curriculum/english-ielts-blueprint.ts`

## Subject

- English

## Year 7

Focus: English foundations with light IELTS alignment.

1. Grammar Foundations A
   - Present simple and present continuous
   - Past simple and past continuous
   - Present perfect essentials
   - Future plans and predictions
   - Questions and short answers
   - Review and mastery check
2. Grammar Foundations B
   - Modals for daily communication
   - Countable nouns, articles, and quantity
   - Pronouns and determiners
   - Adjectives, adverbs, and word order
   - Basic conjunctions and prepositions
3. Vocabulary for Everyday Life
   - Family and relationships
   - Daily routines
   - Food and home
   - Travel and education
   - Work, media, and technology
4. Natural English Phrases
   - Collocations basics
   - Phrasal verbs basics
   - Idioms for school and feelings
   - Agreement, opinion, and response language
5. Reading and Speaking Basics
   - Reading strategies: skimming and paragraph focus
   - Reading strategies: summary and short-answer work
   - Speaking basics on daily life
   - Speaking basics on family and routine
6. Guided Writing
   - Short personal writing and messages
   - Formal apology and request writing
   - First opinion paragraphs
   - Year-end writing portfolio

## Year 8

Focus: academic communication and structured practice.

1. Grammar for Clear Ideas
   - Passive and reported speech
   - Verb patterns with `-ing` and `to`
   - Relative clauses and extra information
   - Comparing, qualifying, and emphasizing
   - Linkers and logic in sentences
2. Intermediate Vocabulary for Study and Society
   - Education and work
   - Technology and environment
   - Media and politics
   - Arts and psychology
   - Global issues and abstract ideas
   - Community, travel, and home
3. Collocations for Academic English
   - Learning how collocations work
   - Study, learning, and presentations
   - Work, business, and academic writing
   - Society, news, money, and global problems
   - Agreeing, deciding, and cause/effect
4. Phrasal Verbs and Idioms for Fluency
   - Classroom and study phrasal verbs
   - Work, business, and telephoning phrasal verbs
   - Communication and problem-solving phrasal verbs
   - Idioms for discussion and response
   - Idioms for time, memory, and communication
5. Reading Skills Lab
   - Paragraph matching and multiple choice
   - Summary completion and short answers
   - Writer viewpoint and argument reading
   - Timed reading mini mock
6. Writing Foundations
   - General Task 1 letters
   - Academic Task 1 charts and graphs
   - Academic Task 1 processes and maps
   - Academic Task 2 opinion and discussion essays
   - Feedback and redrafting cycle
7. Speaking Foundations
   - Part 1 fluency on study and daily life
   - Part 2 cue-card building
   - Part 3 extended discussion
   - Recorded speaking review

## Year 9

Focus: IELTS-aligned preparation track.

1. Advanced Grammar for Accuracy
   - Tense review for narrative and explanation
   - Modals, conditionals, and wish structures
   - Reference, clauses, and sentence control
   - Passive, reporting, and formal expression
   - Editing clinic
2. Advanced Vocabulary Mastery
   - Education, work, and media
   - Technology, science, and environment
   - Politics, society, and global issues
   - Abstract ideas, psychology, and arts
   - Travel, housing, and personal extension bank
3. Advanced Phrase and Idiom Control
   - Advanced collocations for essays and reports
   - Advanced collocations for social issues and public topics
   - Advanced phrasal verbs for study, work, and argument
   - Advanced phrasal verbs for real-world themes
   - Advanced idioms for media, society, and discussion
   - Advanced conversation idioms
4. IELTS Reading Intensive
   - Full reading practice set 1
   - Timed second-cycle reading practice
   - Shorter mock reading practice
   - Weakness review by question type
5. IELTS Writing Intensive
   - Academic Task 1 report cycle
   - Academic Task 2 argument cycle
   - Academic Task 2 cause, solution, and public issue cycle
   - General Task 1 letter cycle
   - General Task 2 essay cycle
   - Timed mock writing with feedback
6. IELTS Speaking Intensive
   - Part 1 topic bank
   - Part 2 cue-card bank A
   - Part 2 cue-card bank B
   - Part 3 abstract discussion
   - Mock speaking and evaluator review
7. Listening Strategy and Resource Lab
   - Prediction and distractors
   - Synonyms, spelling, and answer transfer
   - External listening practice log
8. Mock Exams and Capstone
   - Reading mock
   - Writing mock
   - Speaking mock
   - Final reflection and study plan
